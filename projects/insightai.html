InsightAI

Category AI • Research • Product
Status Portfolio Product

Overview

InsightAI is an interactive AI-style business research assistant designed to transform business ideas into structured insights, opportunities, customer analysis, risks, marketing ideas and action plans.

My Role

Product Lead • Project Supervisor.

I defined the concept, target experience, research workflow, information architecture, features, interface direction and product requirements.

DeepSeek/Codex assisted with implementation. I reviewed, tested and refined the resulting product.

Core Workflow

Business Idea → Customers → Problems → Opportunities → Market → Marketing → Risks → Recommendations → Action Plan

Key Features

Opportunity analysis Customer analysis Problem identification Market assessment Marketing opportunities Risk identification Recommendations Four-week action plan

Biggest Challenge

Designing the research-to-insight workflow so the application felt like one coherent product rather than unrelated AI outputs.

Another challenge was presenting large amounts of strategic information without overwhelming the user.

What I'm Most Proud Of

The business research dashboard that moves the user from understanding an idea toward deciding what to do next.

Technology

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript

Future Development

  • Real AI API
  • Real market data
  • User accounts
  • Saved research
  • Research history
  • Exportable reports
  • Advanced analytics
  • Personalized recommendations
  • Collaboration

Outcome

An interactive business research assistant that demonstrates product thinking and AI-style interface design through a working web application.

What I Learned

Designing an AI-style product requires thinking carefully about the user journey. The research workflow needed to feel logical and useful, not just a collection of generated outputs. Presenting strategic information clearly and helping users make decisions from that information was the core design challenge.

Important: The current version is a portfolio demonstration and uses simulated/AI-style outputs. It should not be represented as verified market research.
