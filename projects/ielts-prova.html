IELTS Prova

Category Founder • EdTech • AI • Product
Status Active Product / Early-Stage Product

Overview

IELTS Prova is an online IELTS preparation platform being developed by Prova Technologies to help students prepare across Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking through structured learning experiences, practice and future AI-powered capabilities.

My Role

Founder & CEO of Prova Technologies.

Responsibilities include product vision, user research, product structure, feature prioritization, roadmap development, UX direction, business strategy, development direction, testing and iteration.

AI-assisted development tools such as DeepSeek/Codex are used to accelerate implementation. I define requirements and specifications, review implementations, test the product and guide iterations.

Problem

IELTS candidates need accessible, structured preparation that allows them to practice different skills, monitor progress and identify areas for improvement.

Objective

Build a digital IELTS preparation experience combining structured practice, progress tracking and future AI-powered learning.

Key Features

Reading practice Listening practice Writing practice Speaking practice Progress tracking Target band tracking Vocabulary Study tasks Study streak Score tracking Premium learning concepts

Biggest Challenge

Designing an experience that combines four IELTS skills while keeping the platform simple and easy to navigate. Another challenge is balancing the long-term product vision with realistic MVP priorities.

What I'm Most Proud Of

Moving IELTS Prova from an idea into a working product concept with a defined structure, branding, product direction, business model and roadmap.

Technology

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript AI-Assisted Development

Future Development

  • AI speaking
  • Personalized learning
  • Expanded question bank
  • Student accounts
  • Advanced analytics
  • Premium subscriptions
  • AI writing/speaking feedback
  • Prova Certification
  • Expansion into other exams

Outcome

A working IELTS preparation platform concept with a defined product structure and roadmap for future development.

What I Learned

Building a product from idea to working concept requires balancing vision with practical execution. Defining clear MVP priorities and focusing on core features first allows for faster iteration and user feedback.

Note: This product is under active development. Some features may be in progress or planned for future releases.
