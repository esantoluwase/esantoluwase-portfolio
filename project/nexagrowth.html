NexaGrowth

Category Web • Business • Strategy
Status Portfolio Concept

Overview

NexaGrowth is a digital growth and technology studio concept demonstrating how web development, digital marketing, business strategy, research and technology strategy can work together.

My Role

Founder • Product Strategist • Project Supervisor.

I defined the concept, positioning, services, target audience, user experience and overall direction.

DeepSeek/Codex assisted with implementation based on my specifications. I reviewed, tested and guided iterations.

Core Concept

Discover → Plan → Build → Grow

Key Areas

Web development Digital marketing Business strategy Market research Technology strategy

Biggest Challenge

Making the project communicate multiple capabilities without making the website feel unfocused.

What I'm Most Proud Of

The way NexaGrowth connects technology and business strategy:

What should we build? → Why? → Who is it for? → How should it work? → How can it grow?

Technology

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript

Future Development

  • Client/project management
  • Marketing analytics
  • Lead generation
  • Content planning
  • Real research capabilities
  • Client dashboards
  • Backend infrastructure
  • AI-powered strategy tools

Outcome

A professional studio concept website that demonstrates the ability to position a service-based business, communicate multiple capabilities and design a premium digital presence.

What I Learned

Creating a business concept and communicating it effectively through a website requires thinking about positioning, audience and value proposition before writing any code. The visual design and content needed to work together to tell a coherent story about what the business does and who it helps.
